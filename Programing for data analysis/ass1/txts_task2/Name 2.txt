Technology changes the way people study and work. Many students now use laptops for most of their assignments. Online platforms make it easier to share files and communicate with teachers. Search engines provide quick access to large amounts of information. However, students still need to evaluate whether information is reliable. Artificial intelligence can also help explain difficult topics. It can provide examples and suggest different ways to solve a problem. At the same time, learners should understand the material themselves. Technology is most useful when it supports learning rather than replacing it. Good digital skills are becoming important in many professions. People also need to understand basic privacy and security. These skills can make everyday work more efficient.
