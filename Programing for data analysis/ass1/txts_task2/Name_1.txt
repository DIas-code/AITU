The morning started quietly, and the streets were almost empty. A small cafe opened near the corner before most shops. People slowly began arriving for coffee and breakfast. The weather was cool, but the sky was clear. A student sat near the window and opened a laptop. He planned to finish several tasks before lunch. Music played softly in the background. Outside, buses carried people toward the city center. By ten o'clock, the cafe became much busier. The student finished his work and ordered another drink. He checked his notes one last time. Then he packed his laptop and left the cafe.
