Clean energy is becoming an important part of modern energy systems. Solar panels generate electricity from sunlight. Wind turbines use moving air to produce electrical power. Both technologies can reduce dependence on fossil fuels. However, renewable energy production can change during the day. Batteries can store extra electricity for later use. Smart grids can help balance production and consumption. Local energy systems may also be useful for remote communities. Building these systems requires engineers, companies, governments, and local residents. Initial installation costs can sometimes be high. Continued technological development may reduce some of these costs. Renewable energy can therefore play an important role in future electricity systems.
