A group of friends decided to spend Saturday outside the city. They left early in the morning to avoid traffic. Their destination was a small lake surrounded by hills. The road took about two hours. When they arrived, the weather was warm and sunny. They found a quiet place near the water and prepared some food. Two people went for a short walk along the lake. The others stayed behind and played cards. In the afternoon, clouds appeared over the hills. The group packed their things before the rain started. They drove back to the city in the evening. Everyone was tired, but the trip had been enjoyable.
