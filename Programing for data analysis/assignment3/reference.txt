Music plagiarism has become a serious problem because huge numbers of tracks are distributed online, while manual checking by experts is slow and subjective. The five articles describe ways to detect it automatically. One system takes polyphonic audio, extracts the melody, converts it into MIDI notes and finds plagiarized fragments with string matching. Another searches a melody database using a similarity model that allows alignment and shifting, a multidimensional index and three-step query processing, which is about 31 times faster than a sequential scan. A third approach builds audio fingerprints from MFCC, entropy and energy features and compares them by Euclidean distance. The authors also describe types of plagiarism: sampling, which is related to cover song detection, rhythm copying such as the Amen Break, and melody copying, which is close to query-by-humming but needs a more precise comparison.
